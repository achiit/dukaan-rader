SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

Operating principles

RF authorization

Use only frequencies, bandwidths, and power levels permitted by the relevant national authority.

Hardware safety

Independent transmit-power interlock and transmit-inhibit controls must be enforced in hardware.

Privacy by design

Display kinematic tracks only. No imagery, biometrics, facial recognition, or personal identity inference.

Cybersecurity

Use authenticated TLS interfaces, role-based access, audit logs, and secure configuration management.
